Meadowlark Cottage Garden

Earn $60 before the end of day 5. Move with WASD or arrow keys. Press 1 for the hoe, 2 for the watering can, or 3 for the scythe; left-click a nearby plot to use your tool and right-click to plant a seed. Press E near the market to sell your harvest or near the cottage to sleep and begin the next day. Press R to play again after the game ends.
